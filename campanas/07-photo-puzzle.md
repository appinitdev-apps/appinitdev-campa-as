## Prompt para ChatGPT: fotografía de referencia

Adjunta **tu fotografía autorizada de la modelo adulta** como referencia al crear la imagen. No cargues capturas de la app para que una IA las redibuje: las incorporarás después como capturas reales.

```text
Using the attached authorized reference portrait of the same ADULT model, produce ONE highly realistic vertical 9:16 photograph (1080x1920 composition). Preserve recognizable facial identity, realistic body proportions and natural skin texture. Do not change the person. Wardrobe: Vestido casual amarillo mostaza y tenis blancos; media coleta. Body pose and hands: Sentada en sillón luminoso, sostiene el móvil con ambas manos y sonríe al resolver una pieza. Scene: Sala moderna con cuadro fotográfico y plantas. Professional naturalistic fashion/lifestyle lighting, full person or medium shot with enough negative space at top for captions. No text, logos, watermarks, application interfaces, floating phones, invented UI, distorted fingers, or duplicate limbs. The reference model must be an adult and used with permission. Produce an authentic believable visual, no exaggerations.
```

## Prompt para Kling AI: animar la foto sin reeditarla

Sube esa imagen a **Image to Video**, usa un clip aproximado de **5 s**, relación vertical 9:16 y movimiento moderado. Evita pedir cambios de rostro, ropa o escenografía.

```text
Animate only this supplied still photograph using Image-to-Video into a 5-second, vertical 9:16, realistic clip. Keep the same adult person, face, exact outfit, body shape, hairstyle, skin tone, surroundings and lighting from first to last frame. Motion: A tiny delighted smile after looking down at the phone, gentle camera pan, preserve fingers and phone details. Gentle natural breathing and blinking, minimal gestures, stable hands. No mouth speaking or lip sync, no new objects, no text, no logos, no camera jumps, no outfit changes or identity drift.
```
