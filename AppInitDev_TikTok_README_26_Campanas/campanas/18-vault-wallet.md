## Prompt para Kling AI: animar la foto sin reeditarla

Sube esa imagen a **Image to Video**, usa un clip aproximado de **5 s**, relación vertical 9:16 y movimiento moderado. Evita pedir cambios de rostro, ropa o escenografía.

```text
Animate only this supplied still photograph using Image-to-Video into a 5-second, vertical 9:16, realistic clip. Keep the same adult person, face, exact outfit, body shape, hairstyle, skin tone, surroundings and lighting from first to last frame. Motion: Look down at paper budget then phone, brief confident smile, gentle camera slide. Gentle natural breathing and blinking, minimal gestures, stable hands. No mouth speaking or lip sync, no new objects, no text, no logos, no camera jumps, no outfit changes or identity drift.
```
